How this works. Scores come from ESPN's public feed. Draw sheets — the slot order, seeds and byes that make the bracket a tree rather than a list — plus each event's tour level and surface come from Wikipedia, so they're crowdsourced and occasionally out of date; a draw is only used once it agrees with the live results about who plays whom. Spotted something wrong? It's fixable at the source. The player numbers are computed from the crowdsourced Match Charting Project. The small-print win probability is experimental: it comes from each player's charted serve and return rates, and it stays deliberately simple — surface and recent-form adjustments were tested against held-out results and don't improve it at this data resolution. It is only as good as the charting behind it.
